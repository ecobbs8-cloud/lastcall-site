Privacy policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Last Call is a cocktail match-3 game for adults. It is made and published by the developer of Last Call, an independent developer listed as the seller on the App Store ("we", "us"). We decide how the personal data described here is used, so we are the controller of it.

This policy covers the Last Call iOS app.

The short version: we have no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of our own that receive your game. Your save stays on your device. A few trusted partners (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat) receive limited data so that leaderboards, purchases, and ads work. We do not sell your personal data.

If you have a question or want to use any of your rights, write to lastcallmatch3.support@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

These things are saved only in local storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Your night progress, stars, and scores
  • Settings and your chosen language
  • The recipe book and bar points
  • Items and themes you have unlocked
  • Your "yes" answer to the 18+ question

We do not upload this save file, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Data that is collected, and how

From you, when you contact us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. This happens outside the app.

Through Apple Game Center, if you are signed in. After the 18+ question, the app asks Game Center to sign you in quietly. If you are signed in, Apple receives your Game Center player name and player ID together with the scores you finish: the daily board, the week, and your best night. These scores appear on Game Center leaderboards that other players can see. If you decline or are not signed in, your scores stay on the device.

Through your purchase. Remove Ads + Starter Bundle is a one-time purchase (product id bar.lastcall.remove_ads_starter) sold by Apple. Apple handles payment, so we never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID that it creates and the App Store purchase record (product, date, price, currency, and store country). This lets Restore Purchases give the bundle back on a new or reset device.

Through ads, collected by Google. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. When the AdMob software runs on your device, Google may collect:

  • Device and app details, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and the ID for this app on this device (IDFV)
  • Your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them
  • Your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, or browsing history. We do not use a crash reporter or a product analytics service.

4. How we use data

  • To run the game you asked for: keeping your progress on the device, posting your scores to Game Center leaderboards, and delivering and restoring your purchase.
  • To show ads that keep the game free: ads are requested only after you confirm you are 18 or older. A full-screen ad can play between nights, only after you win a night, on the way to the next night or the menu. It never plays during a shift, after a loss, on a retry, in your first session, before night 4, or when you return to the app, and ads are spaced at least two nights and three minutes apart. Rewarded videos are always your choice: a start booster, +15 seconds after a near miss, or doubling the daily gift. Buying Remove Ads stops the between-night ads and gives you the rewards without a video.
  • To personalize ads, only with your permission: Google uses the IDFA for more relevant ads only if you allow tracking, and in regions that require it, only with your choice in Google's consent form.
  • To answer you: replying to your emails and handling privacy requests.
  • To keep things safe and legal: preventing fraud with purchases, and meeting tax, accounting, and other legal duties.

We do not build profiles about you ourselves, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: delivering and restoring your purchase, and Game Center leaderboards you choose to use.
  • Consent: personalized ads, use of the IDFA, and the storing or reading of information on your device for advertising. You give or refuse this through the iOS tracking prompt and Google's consent form, and you can withdraw it at any time (see section 8).
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalized ads to fund a free game, answering support emails, and preventing fraud. We think these interests are not outweighed by your rights, and you can object to them.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records needed for tax and accounting law.

8. Your choices

Política de privacidad

Quiénes somos

Last Call es un match-3 de cócteles para adultos, creado por el desarrollador de Last Call, un desarrollador independiente que figura como vendedor en la App Store. Somos responsables de los datos descritos aquí. Esta política cubre la app de iOS Last Call. No hay cuentas y no vendemos tus datos. Vigente desde el 14 de noviembre de 2026.

Edad

La app abre con una pregunta de 18+. Un «no» dura solo esa sesión y no se guarda. No se pide ningún anuncio antes de tu «sí», y los anuncios se limitan a contenido para adultos. No recogemos a sabiendas datos de menores de 18 años. Si crees que ha pasado, escríbenos y los borraremos.

En tu dispositivo

El progreso, las puntuaciones, los ajustes, el idioma, el recetario, los puntos del bar, los objetos y temas desbloqueados y el «sí» de la edad se quedan en el almacenamiento local de tu dispositivo. No subimos esa partida y no podemos verla. Borrar la app la borra. No hay informes de fallos ni servicio de analítica.

Game Center

Después de la pregunta de 18+, la app pide a Apple Game Center que inicie tu sesión en silencio. Si tienes la sesión iniciada, Apple recibe tu nombre e ID de Game Center con las puntuaciones que terminas: el tablero del día, la semana y tu mejor noche. Otros jugadores las ven en las clasificaciones. Si dices que no o no has iniciado sesión, tus puntuaciones se quedan en el dispositivo.

Compras

Sin anuncios + pack inicial es una compra única (id bar.lastcall.remove_ads_starter) vendida por Apple, así que nunca vemos tu tarjeta. RevenueCat recibe un ID de usuario anónimo y el recibo de la App Store para que Restaurar compras funcione. Ese recibo sirve para entregar tu compra y para la contabilidad, no para anuncios.

Anuncios

Google AdMob muestra anuncios en la versión gratuita. Un anuncio a pantalla completa solo puede salir entre noches, después de ganar una noche. Nunca durante un turno, después de perder, en un reintento, en tu primera sesión ni antes de la noche 4, y entre dos anuncios pasan al menos dos noches y tres minutos. Los vídeos con premio solo salen si los tocas: impulso inicial, +15 segundos tras un casi fallo, o regalo del día doble. Sin anuncios quita los anuncios entre noches y da los premios sin vídeo.

Datos de anuncios y tus opciones

Cuando hay anuncios, Google puede recoger datos del dispositivo y de la app, tu dirección IP (que puede indicar una ubicación aproximada) y cómo interactúas con los anuncios. Google recibe tu identificador de publicidad (IDFA) solo si permites el rastreo en el aviso de iOS. Si dices que no, sigues jugando y no volvemos a preguntar. Puedes cambiarlo en Ajustes de iOS, Privacidad y seguridad, y luego Rastreo. En el EEE, el Reino Unido y Suiza, el formulario de consentimiento de Google aparece antes de pedir un anuncio. Para cambiar esa elección, borra y reinstala la app, o escríbenos.
