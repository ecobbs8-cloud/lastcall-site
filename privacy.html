Privacy policy

Who we are

Last Call is a cocktail match-3 for adults. This policy describes the shipping iOS app. The web build is a labeled preview and does not charge a card.

Age

The app opens with an 18+ gate. A “no” is kept only for that session and is not written to disk. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we do not request ads before the gate is accepted.

On this device

Night progress, settings, language, the recipe book, bar points, and the age “yes” stay in local storage on the device. We do not run an account system, and we do not upload that save file.

Game Center

After the 18+ check, the iOS app asks Apple Game Center to sign in quietly. If you accept, Apple receives your Game Center player name and the scores you finish: that day’s shared board, the week, and your best night. If you decline or are not signed in, those scores stay on this device. There is still no Last Call account.

Purchases

Remove Ads + Starter Bundle is a one-time non-consumable, product id bar.lastcall.remove_ads_starter, sold through Apple at the price Apple shows. RevenueCat, if the public iOS key is configured, receives an anonymous app user id and the purchase record so Restore Purchases can work. That record is used to deliver the purchase, not to advertise, and it is not sold.

Ads

Forced ads run only between nights, and only after the limits already in the game. A rewarded ad can grant +15 seconds on a near miss, or an optional tool. If you bought Remove Ads, those videos are skipped and the reward is granted. Google AdMob may receive an advertising identifier (IDFA) only after you allow tracking in the iOS prompt. If you refuse, we still let you play and we do not keep asking. In the EU and UK, Google’s consent form (UMP) is shown before an ad request. Ad requests use a mature content ceiling. Nothing is sold.

Questions and deletion requests for purchase data held by RevenueCat: erick.cobbs@mail.grokbot.com. There is no Last Call account to delete.
