The game

Last Call: Match 3 is a cocktail match-3, not a jewel grid. You swap bottles, pour real recipes and serve every ticket before closing time. 58 nights across two seasons ship at launch. Season 2 opens after Season 1 with new ingredients, harder goals and four boss nights. A jukebox holds 10 original songs you earn by winning nights or buy with bar points. Named combos, Happy Hour, regulars, and a signature drink you design yourself. A shared Daily Board with a streak, plus a seven-night gift calendar. Tuned haptics. On iPad and iPhone Duo the bar opens into a wide layout. Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Fair optional ads. No subscription or energy timer.
